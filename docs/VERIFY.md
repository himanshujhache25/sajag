## Facts

1. SEBI FY25-FY26 equity derivatives studies: the 87.7% net-loss figure for
   FY26, the 91% figure for FY25, the repeat-loss figure near 90%, and the 97%
   options-buyer figure. Check the exact wording and the publication date.
2. SEBI advisory of 21 May 2025 (PR 27/2025) on "VIP" WhatsApp and Telegram
   groups.
3. SEBI press release of 8 April 2025 (PR 20/2025) on the 1600 number series
   for service and transactional calls to existing customers. Sales calls are
   not covered; confirm that reading.
4. SEBI press release of 11 June 2025 (PR 31/2025) on `@valid` UPI handles,
   the `.brk` and `.mf` category parts, the 1 October 2025 date, and that
   `@valid` is an additional option, not a replacement.
5. SCORES 2.0: the 21 calendar day reply window, the two levels of review, and
   the statement about dealings with unregistered intermediaries.
6. 1930: the acknowledgement number and the 24-hour window to complete details
   on the cybercrime portal.
7. Registration prefixes. `INZ`, `INH`, `INA`, `INP`, `INM`, `IN-DP` are used.
   `INB`, `INF` and `INR` are **not** recognised until confirmed; the engine
   must report them as "unknown category, check on SEBI".
8. The exact sentence that tips and personal advice need an RA (INH) or an
   IA (INA) registration.
9. The standard disclosure that registration does not guarantee performance
   or returns.
